●▲●▲●▲ — what comes next? Most people can answer "●!" right away. Noticing what comes next by looking at repeating shapes or colors is what mathematicians call finding a pattern. It looks simple, but it's actually the beginning of some very important thinking skills.

Finding a pattern means spotting "the smallest chunk that repeats." In ●▲●▲●▲, the repeating chunk is '●▲' — just two shapes. Once you spot that chunk, you can predict the next spot, and the one after that, forever. Longer chunks are a bit trickier, but the idea of "finding the repeat" stays exactly the same. Try different difficulty levels to take on a variety of patterns.
