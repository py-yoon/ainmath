Flat Shapes: Count the Sides and Corners

Early elementary · About 3 min read

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Read this together with a grown-up. Some words may still be tricky to read alone. Reading it out loud together makes it easier to understand.

Triangle, square, circle — the first shapes kids ever meet. But why is a triangle called a "triangle"? The answer is very simple.

A triangle has 3 straight lines, called sides. It also has 3 pointy points, called corners (or vertices). That's why it's a tri-angle. A square has 4 sides and 4 corners.

A "side" is a straight line that makes up part of a shape's outline. A "corner" is the pointy spot where two sides meet. A triangle's 3 sides meet to form 3 corners.

A four-sided shape (square or rectangle) has 4 sides forming 4 corners. A five-sided shape (pentagon) has 5, and a six-sided shape (hexagon) has 6. For these shapes, the number of sides and the number of corners are always the same.

Each dot is a corner, and the straight line between two dots is a side

A circle is a little different. It has no straight sides. It has no pointy corners either. It's made of one single curved line that loops all the way around.

That's why a circle is a different kind of shape from a triangle or a square. Ask your child, "why does a circle look so different from a triangle or a square?" and let them find the difference between straight lines and curved lines themselves.

The more sides a shape has, the rounder it looks, closer and closer to a circle

Shapes like these are hiding all around us. Road signs are triangles or circles. Windows and books are rectangles. A honeycomb is made of hexagons.

Take a walk around the house or the neighborhood with your child and ask, "what shape is this?" It helps shapes feel less like pictures in a book and more like familiar things in everyday life.

The best way to practice is to point at each side with a finger and count "one, two, three." It also helps to cut colored paper into triangles and squares and stick a sticker on every corner. Counting the sides and corners with your own hands makes it click why each shape got its name.
